Please start by clicking on [Sign in with HPC-NET]
![](images/waldur-login_laife.png)
  
By selecting the "Latvian Academic Identity Federation (LAIFE)" method, you should be able to authenticate with your university login details.
![](images/waldur-keycloak1_laife.png)
  
In LAIFE window under "Latvia" list search for your university's identity provider. In case of the Riga Technical University, select identity provider "Riga Technical University".
![](images/waldur-laife0.png)
  
Similar to University of Latvia  
![](images/waldur-laife0_2.png)

and Riga Stradiņš University  
![](images/waldur-laife0_1.png)

You will be redirected to your organization's identity provider authorization portal, which in this case is RTU ORTUS. Please follow all authentication steps, beginning with your login credentials:
![](images/waldur-laife1.png)

After successfully authenticating with your organization's identity provider, you will be presented with the LAIFE Transferred Information page. Click the "Remember" button, and then select "Yes, continue" to proceed.
![](images/waldur-laife2.png)

After that, you will see an authentication warning message. Please click the "Add to existing account" option.
![](images/waldur-keycloak2_laife.png)

You will be redirected to the next page, where you will see information on how to merge your account with LAIFE.
![](images/waldur-keycloak3_laife.png)

You will receive an email from hpc@rtu.lv. In the email, click "Link to confirm account linking" to open the confirmation link.
![](images/waldur-keycloak4_laife.png)

After opening the link, you will see a confirmation page where you need to click "Click here to proceed".
![](images/waldur-keycloak5_laife.png)

On the next page, you will see a message confirming that your email address has been successfully verified.

![](images/waldur-keycloak6_laife.png)

After that, return to the previous page and click "If you already verified the email in a different browser".
![](images/waldur-keycloak7_laife.png)

After successful authentication you will be asked to confirm[ACCEPT] you name and email in Waldur system profile:
![](images/waldur-project_laife.png)
